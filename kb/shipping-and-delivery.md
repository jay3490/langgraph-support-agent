# Shipping and delivery times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after an order leaves our warehouse.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

When an order ships you receive an email with the carrier name and a tracking number.
Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear on the carrier's website.

If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or the parcel is marked delivered but
you cannot find it, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.
