# Refund policy

Delivered orders can be refunded within 30 days of delivery. Gold members have an
extended 45-day refund window.

Digital products such as software licences, and gift cards, are not refundable. If an
order mixes refundable and non-refundable items, only the refundable items count
towards the refund amount.

Refunds above the approval limit are reviewed by a member of the billing team before
they are issued. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and usually
appear on your statement within 5 to 10 business days.

Orders that have not been delivered yet cannot be refunded. Cancel the order instead,
or wait until it arrives and then request a refund.
